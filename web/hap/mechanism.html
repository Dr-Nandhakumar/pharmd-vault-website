ABOUT THIS ATLAS

Your syllabus, made explorable.

Built around the five units of BP104T: Human Anatomy, Physiology and Pathophysiology I. All 102 mapped topics include study notes, a mechanism sequence and a question.

Models are teaching schematics, not patient-specific anatomy. Related topics share a relevant model. Animations demonstrate selected relationships and are not quantitative simulations.

The cell and membrane models were created in Blender. Other functional teaching diagrams use Three.js. Narration uses voices available in your browser.

Reading sources
