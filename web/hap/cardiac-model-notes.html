Cardiac laboratory: model and limits

This original educational implementation couples a time-varying left-ventricular elastance with a two-element arterial Windkessel. It is not a validated patient model, a full cardiovascular simulation, or a disease predictor. The 3D atlas supplies static anatomical context; highlights locate structures and do not represent deformation or moving valve leaflets.

Equations

PLV = E(t) × (V − V₀)
Qmitral = max(0, (Patrium − PLV) / Rmitral)
Qaortic = max(0, (PLV − Paorta) / Raortic)
dV/dt = Qmitral − Qaortic
dPaorta/dt = (Qaortic − (Paorta − Pvenous)/Rsystemic) / Carterial

Stroke volume is the integrated forward aortic flow per beat. Cardiac output is stroke volume × heart rate, converted from mL/min to L/min. Ejection fraction is (EDV − ESV)/EDV × 100. Valve states follow pressure gradients.

Illustrative assumptions

  • Constant atrial pressure: no atrial contraction or atrial volume state.
  • Passive elastance 0.08 mmHg/mL; reference volume 10 mL; arterial compliance 1.5 mL/mmHg; venous pressure 5 mmHg; each valve resistance 0.02 mmHg·s/mL.
  • A smooth sin² activation has an illustrative 0.28-second duration, capped at 45% of the cycle. It is not a measured activation waveform.
  • Ideal one-way valves: no regurgitation, leaflet mechanics, inertia or stenosis model.
  • Forward Euler integration uses 3,200 steps per beat, with at least 30 seconds of equilibration before recording a cycle.
  • No right ventricle, pulmonary circulation, ECG, autonomic reflexes, coronary perfusion or patient-specific properties.
  • The slider bounds are teaching limits, not clinical reference ranges. Photorealistic textures and anatomical motion are not provided by this model.

Verification

Automated tests cover 81 combinations of the displayed parameter endpoints and baseline values, conservation of ventricular volume, periodic convergence, one-way flow and valve states, expected directional responses, invalid inputs, and time-step convergence. These are numerical checks, not experimental or clinical validation.
